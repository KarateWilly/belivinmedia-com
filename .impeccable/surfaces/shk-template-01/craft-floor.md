## Render artifact contract

- Final desktop hero: native landscape generation with high-resolution pass enabled, composed for the approved desktop layout rather than cropped afterward.
- Final mobile hero: independent portrait generation with high-resolution pass enabled, composed for the approved mobile layout rather than derived from desktop.
- Supporting section images: generated to each section's actual aspect ratio after layout placement is known.
- Final images must be visually approved before HTML/CSS polish. CSS may stage an approved asset but may not hide blur, implausible equipment, awkward anatomy, bad text, weak framing or a wrong subject.
- Actual dimensions, encoding and output file size are recorded after generation. A nominal model setting is not accepted as proof of resolution.

## Craft risk

Primary risk: generated domestic SHK imagery becomes industrial, technically implausible, generic stock photography or an image of equipment instead of a homeowner outcome. Secondary risk: literal commissioning paperwork overtakes the conversion hierarchy.

## Non-negotiable visual QA

For every final asset inspect the pixels, not only metadata:

1. homeowner/family context reads naturally and is not a staged stock-photo pose;
2. hands, faces, eyes, tools and technician posture are anatomically credible;
3. installation belongs in the depicted room and German residential context;
4. no outdoor heat-pump fan unit appears indoors;
5. pipe routes, manifolds, valves, gauges, fixtures and appliance clearances are plausible;
6. no generated text, pseudo-logo, fake certification or brand mark appears on clothing, equipment or walls;
7. the quiet text zone remains clean at the final desktop or mobile crop;
8. important faces, hands and equipment are not hidden by the planned HTML layer;
9. light, materials and color grade remain consistent across the page;
10. the image passes a human visual review at 100% and at its browser render size.

## Taste preflight applied

- Hero fits initial viewport; headline max two lines desktop; subcopy max 20 words; CTA visible.
- No three-equal-card row, repeated split sections, decorative crosshair grids, fake precision, ratings, badges, outdoor-unit errors, glass or gradient slop.
- One accent color; one radius logic; one contact label per intent.
- Eyebrow maximum one per three sections.
- Mobile collapse is explicit per section.
- Motion communicates hierarchy or state and honors reduced motion.
- Hero image is preloaded; dimensions are reserved; LCP, CLS, INP and Lighthouse are tested before release.
