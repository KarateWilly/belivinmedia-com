# Audience and Offer Research - German SHK B2C

Research date: 2026-08-11

## Question

What should a generic German SHK showroom template actually target and show if it is meant to demonstrate a strong B2C website rather than a B2B/industrial one?

## Primary evidence

### ZVSHK Serviceportal SHK

Source: https://www.zvshk.de/themen/serviceportal-shk

The industry association describes digital consumer requests around concrete conditions in the `Eigenheim`. Named service paths include heating maintenance, drinking-water installation and air filters. The portal is explicitly designed to generate and qualify direct consumer enquiries for SHK guild businesses.

**Implication:** Private residential intent is real and broad. It includes maintenance and hygiene, not only large renovation projects.

### Das Handwerk occupational profile

Source: https://www.handwerk.de/infos-zur-ausbildung/ausbildungsberufe/berufsprofile/anlagenmechanikerin-fuer-sanitaer-heizungs-und-klimatechnik

The official craft profile states that SHK mechanics equip and support private households and businesses with water connections, heating and sanitary installations. It names heating, climate and ventilation systems, modern bathrooms and solar systems, with the core activity pattern `install`, `maintain`, `advise`.

**Implication:** A generic template needs to communicate installation, ongoing service and understandable advice. A single heating-only or bathroom-only identity is too narrow.

### BIBB modernized SHK profile

Source: https://www.bibb.de/de/pressemitteilung_50243.php

BIBB groups the occupation into sanitary technology, heating technology, ventilation and air-conditioning, renewables and environmental technology. It treats the house as an energetic system, highlights drinking-water and ventilation hygiene, and explicitly emphasizes commissioning, handover and customer instruction.

**Implication:** `Inbetriebnahme-Pass` is culturally grounded in the actual work. It must represent the whole home system and customer understanding rather than industrial equipment.

### ZVSHK 2024 balance

Source: https://www.zvshk.de/presse/medien-center/pressemitteilungen/shk-handwerk-mit-verhaltener-bilanz-2024

The association reports uncertainty and a strong decline in new-heating demand in 2024, while customer service and maintenance remained a stable revenue pillar. It also points to modernization support and the bathroom market as strategic demand areas.

**Implication:** The page should not rely on a glamorous one-off heat-pump or dream-bath pitch. Service, modernization and reliability need equal commercial weight.

### VdZ / VDS SHK economic barometer Q1 2026

Source: https://vdzev.de/branche/konjunkturbarometer/

The current barometer covers sanitary, heating, ventilation and climate. It reports that customer service is developing better than sanitary and heating. Heating improved, while sanitary remained weaker because private households delayed non-essential bathroom renovation and new construction remained weak.

**Implication:** The most robust generic website promise is not `new bathroom` or `new heating`. It is competent residential building technology across service and modernization.

### ZVSHK bathroom modernization evidence

Sources:
- https://www.zvshk.de/presse/medien-center/statementdienst/zvshk-setzt-sich-fuer-nachhaltige-badmodernisierung
- https://www.zvshk.de/presse/medien-center/pressemitteilungen/innovationsdialog-barrierefreie-und-pflegegerechte-badgestaltung

ZVSHK reports roughly one million bathroom conversions in 2023 and, in 2025, more than seven million bathrooms considered in urgent need of renovation. The demand includes comfort, hygiene, safety, accessibility and care at home, not merely aesthetic luxury.

**Implication:** Bathroom imagery should feel usable, inclusive and residential rather than luxury-showroom-only. The audience includes older owners and multigenerational households, not only young families.

## Audience conclusion

The template's downstream audience is **private residential decision-makers**:

- owner-occupiers and homeowners;
- couples, families and multigenerational households;
- older owners planning safe or accessible renovation;
- landlords or small residential property owners seeking dependable service;
- people planning renovation, modernization, maintenance or fault resolution.

The showroom visitor is still the SHK business owner evaluating BELIVIN's website quality. The demonstrated site must therefore prove that BELIVIN can speak to the business owner's actual private customers without locking the business to one demographic or service niche.

## Offer architecture for the generic template

1. **Heizung und Energie** - heating modernization, control, efficiency and renewable systems.
2. **Bad und Wasser** - sanitary installation, bathroom modernization, drinking water, comfort and accessibility.
3. **Service und Wartung** - inspection, maintenance, repair and understandable handover.
4. **Klima and ventilation** can enter later or replace a group when the real customer business offers it.

## Copy baseline

- **Headline:** `Wärme. Wasser. Zuhause.`
- **Subcopy:** `Heizung, Bad und Haustechnik für Modernisierung, Sanierung und Service in Ihrer Region.`
- **Primary action:** `Termin anfragen`
- **First-view service signals:** `Heizung & Energie`, `Bad & Wasser`, `Service & Wartung`

## Image baseline

- Residential, lived-in and attainable, not industrial and not luxury-only.
- Show one private-home outcome with credible craft proof.
- A family may appear, but copy and casting must not define the offer as family-only.
- Later sections deliberately broaden representation: finished bathroom/water use, heating/energy work and maintenance/handover.
- No hero equipment fetish. The home and its owner remain the reason the technology exists.

## Composition decision

Use the `Wohnwärme` composition mechanics as the baseline, renamed internally to `Zuhause`. Retain its strong residential outcome and recessed technical proof, but replace the heating-only headline with the broader copy baseline above. Bathroom/water and service receive their own major image moments below the fold.
