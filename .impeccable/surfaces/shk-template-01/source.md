## Selection note

Mario preferred the originality of `Versorgung in Schichten`, but selected `Inbetriebnahme-Pass` for Template 1 because it provides stronger immediate clarity and credibility. The selected world must not inherit the industrial plant-room emphasis of the exploratory sketch. Primary-source research defines the downstream audience as private residential decision-makers rather than a specific family demographic: owner-occupiers, couples, families, older owners, multigenerational households and small residential property owners seeking service, maintenance, modernization or renovation. Final imagery therefore leads with residential life and owner outcomes; technical detail functions as proof.

## Five-part direction contract

### 1. Principles

1. **Home outcome first, technical proof second.** Every major composition begins with the lived residential result: warmth, comfort, a clean modernization, a usable bathroom or a family home. Equipment appears only where it explains the competence behind that result.
2. **Commissioning means trust, not paperwork.** Measurement marks, rules and signed bands communicate care, completion and understandable handover. They must never recreate a bureaucratic form or a literal PDF.
3. **No fabricated proof.** No ratings, awards, customer counts, response-time guarantees, brand partnerships or testimonials without verified evidence.
4. **One decisive action.** The primary contact action is visually singular. The design does not scatter equal-weight buttons through the first viewport.
5. **Domestic truth.** Residential rooms, utility areas, installations, tools, clothing and homeowner behavior must be plausible for German SHK work. No outdoor units indoors, impossible pipework, fantasy controls or generated workwear text.

### 2. Typography

- **Primary voice:** a sturdy humanist grotesk with generous lowercase readability and confident large-scale headlines; candidate family `Manrope`.
- **Technical voice:** a restrained monospaced family for region labels, commissioning marks and service metadata; candidate family `IBM Plex Mono`.
- Headlines use sentence case or short emphatic lines, never condensed all-caps shouting across entire sections.
- Labels remain small but legible, with moderate tracking. They behave like authored handover information, not dashboard telemetry.
- No more than two type families in the final surface.

### 3. Color and material

- **Ground:** warm mineral off-white / limewash, never clinical pure white.
- **Text:** deep graphite rather than industrial black.
- **Secondary material:** brushed residential steel and soft stone gray.
- **Signal:** one restrained vermilion / fired-clay accent for the decisive action and verified state.
- **Warmth:** natural oak, skin tones and warm residential light come from photography, not decorative gradients.
- Texture may appear as mineral paper grain, pencil-thin measurement rules and sparse brushed-metal detail. No glassmorphism, glow, fake chrome or heavy shadows.

### 4. Composition

- Desktop is an authored asymmetrical field, not a generic 50/50 split hero.
- A high-quality residential documentary image owns the emotional majority of the first viewport. The text plane occupies a naturally calm image zone or an adjacent mineral field without trapping every element in a card.
- Brand, homeowner outcome, region, one primary action and three core service signals are visible in the first viewport.
- Technical marks cross section boundaries sparingly to establish continuity; they must not become decorative pipe diagrams.
- Subsequent sections alternate lived result, craft proof and understandable process rather than repeating icon-card grids.
- Image subjects for final assets: broad homeowner context, bright renovation or modernization, credible residential utility spaces, bathroom/water work, heating/energy work and clean service or handover interaction. A family may appear, but the page must not frame the offer as family-only. Avoid large industrial plant rooms as the hero.

### 5. Adaptation

- Mobile is a separately authored composition, not a center crop or stacked desktop.
- A dedicated portrait image places the homeowner/family outcome and technician in a mobile-safe arrangement, leaving a real calm zone for text.
- The mobile first viewport keeps brand, short outcome headline, region cue and primary action visible without covering faces, hands, tools or critical installation detail.
- Technical bands linearize into short signed rows; decorative calibration marks reduce substantially.
- At 390 px the composition must feel intentional; at 320 px it must remain readable with no text bleed, overlap or clipped controls.
- Reduced motion uses static state changes; no meaning depends on parallax.

## Standing alternative

`Versorgung in Schichten` remains a candidate for SHK Template 2. It must not be blended into Template 1; the three templates need separate visual systems rather than a shared hybrid.
