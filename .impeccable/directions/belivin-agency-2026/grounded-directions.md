# BELIVIN Agency 2026 — Direction Round (Gate A)

Surface: `index.html` (main site), mode Persuade. Replacement world: the Handwerk "Auftragszentrale" build is retired and serves only as anti-reference.

Seed key: `67b063e5` (concept-seed, scope direction, mode persuade, catalog pool `c3b204a1eed6`). Assigned index: 3.

## Grounding

- Mechanism: BELIVIN runs a company's whole demand chain (website, ads, social content, search/AI visibility) as one connected system with one team, so every channel feeds the others and leads to booked conversations.
- Audience scene: an owner or marketing lead between meetings, on phone or laptop, comparing a few agencies found via search, social or referral; skeptical of agency fluff.
- Cultural home: German business life plus the media and screen world these people read daily: TV, news, feeds, search results, dashboards.
- First surface must prove: BELIVIN is contemporary and technically capable (the site itself is the proof), what the four services are, and the one action (book a conversation).
- Category default (rut): near-black hero with gradient light or glowing orb, statement headline, service bento grid, client-logo marquee, testimonial slider, "Let's talk" footer.
- Predictable opposite (rut): stark white Swiss/brutalist page, giant type, no imagery, the "anti-agency agency".
- Literal reading of the brief ("techy marketing agency"): campaign dashboard; spent once, as candidate 5.

## Grounded candidates, ordered by resonance (fixed before the roll)

1. **Mischpult / Marketing-Mix-Konsole** (hardware instrument): the textbook term "Marketing-Mix" made literal; four channel strips, one master bus.
2. **Suchergebnis + KI-Antwort** (interface): the visibility battleground itself; the visitor's company rising into the answer.
3. **Sende-Regie** (media production): TV control room with multiviewer wall, vision mixer, program/preview buses, tally lights and timecode; "MEDIA" made literal, all channels watched and cut from one room.
4. **Litfaßsäule / Plakat** (print, out-of-home): German advertising heritage, the classic agency's native medium.
5. **Kampagnen-Leitstand** (interface): ads-manager control dashboard; the brief's literal reading.
6. **Fallblattanzeige** (electromechanical): the station departure board; flaps flip to the next arrival.
7. **Oszilloskop „Signal statt Rauschen“** (lab instrument): phosphor graticule, waveforms locking into a clean signal.

Material families covered: hardware instrument, interface, media production, print, electromechanical, lab instrument.
