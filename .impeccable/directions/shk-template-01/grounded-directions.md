# SHK Template 01 — Grounded Direction Derivation

Method: Analogy and Blending — William J. J. Gordon / Arthur Koestler / Fauconnier & Turner.

## Home problem

A cold SHK lead must recognize the trade, believe the website craft is commercially serious, and find a contact action within seconds on desktop and phone. The site must demonstrate technical competence without looking like generic blue tradesware or a luxury-bath brochure.

## Refused category defaults

- interchangeable split hero with crossed-arm technician and blue CTA;
- dark industrial dashboard with decorative gauges and grid lines;
- black-and-gold luxury bathroom page that hides heating and service work.

## Ordered grounded directions

### 1. Werkbank 360

**Remote structure:** A precision calibration bench where every tool and measured part has a fixed place.

**Mapping:** The brand becomes the workshop nameplate; services occupy real bench zones; contact is the one prominent red workshop stop/submit control. Real overhead and oblique photography shows gauges, copper, graphite insulation and clean tools.

**Emergent mechanism:** The visitor reads order and care before reading claims. Mobile turns the bench into three stacked workstations rather than cropping the full table.

**Risk:** Can feel like tool retail and underrepresent finished living spaces.

### 2. Versorgungslinie

**Remote structure:** German municipal wayfinding that moves people through complex infrastructure with restrained line codes and large location typography.

**Mapping:** Heating, water and service use independent routes through real regional and technical photography. Navigation behaves like destination signage; the call action is the one terminal destination.

**Emergent mechanism:** A complex SHK offer becomes an immediately legible route from household need to service action.

**Risk:** Red/blue lines can become decorative plumbing diagrams or repeat the discarded corridor world.

### 3. Inbetriebnahme-Pass — assigned direction

**Remote structure:** The formal commissioning and handover of a technical building system: inspect, measure, explain, release.

**Mapping:** The first viewport is a documentary SHK scene framed by a clean commissioning field. Company identity, region and phone sit like authored handover data, never a form. Three services are visible as signed system bands. The CTA is the decisive release mark: `Diesen Stil anfragen` in the showroom frame and `Termin anfragen` inside the example flow.

**Emergent mechanism:** Trust comes from visible completion and explainability rather than fabricated ratings. The page demonstrates that good SHK work leaves the customer with an ordered, understandable system.

**Desktop expression:** Wide high-resolution documentary scene with a technician performing a real measurement at a clean residential plant system. A substantial paper/metal information field occupies a deliberately quiet image zone; precise rules, calibration ticks and one functional signal color establish the handover language.

**Mobile expression:** Independently generated portrait scene with technician and measuring point held in the lower half, calm upper field for identity/headline, and three signed service rows in natural reading order. No desktop crop.

**Risk:** If the visual language becomes literal paperwork, the page feels bureaucratic. Photography and type scale must keep it a premium website, not a PDF form.

### 4. Materialbibliothek Wärme / Wasser / Raum

**Remote structure:** An architect's physical material library where copper, ceramic, steel, graphite insulation and water surfaces are judged side by side.

**Mapping:** Each service owns a tactile material family; the website scroll feels like pulling large samples from a wall rather than opening cards.

**Emergent mechanism:** Invisible infrastructure gains physical value through materials customers recognize and touch in real homes.

**Risk:** Can privilege bathroom aesthetics and make service/maintenance look secondary.

### 5. Sichtbar / Thermisch

**Remote structure:** Paired visible-light and thermal inspection imagery used to locate performance differences.

**Mapping:** Hero and service stories show real rooms and their restrained thermal counterpart; typography lives in the divide between what customers see and what technicians diagnose.

**Emergent mechanism:** The page literally demonstrates hidden competence.

**Risk:** Generated thermal imagery can be technically false or look like an energy-consulting gimmick.

### 6. Montagefolge

**Remote structure:** A high-quality exploded assembly manual where sequence, fit and service access are unmistakable.

**Mapping:** Services unfold through large photographed steps, sparse line drawings and direct verbs. The mobile page naturally becomes a vertical build sequence.

**Emergent mechanism:** Process proof replaces testimonial theatre.

**Risk:** Too much diagram language can feel unfinished and exclude customers who want outcomes rather than installation detail.

### 7. Rheingauer Werkstattfenster

**Remote structure:** A local documentary photo essay seen through workshop windows: region outside, craft and materials inside.

**Mapping:** The first viewport joins recognizable regional light with a real technical scene; sections alternate human craft, equipment and finished spaces.

**Emergent mechanism:** Locality and technical credibility occupy one photographic world instead of separate trust sections.

**Risk:** Familiar editorial photography may be less ownable and can drift back toward a conventional agency layout.

## Challenger weighing

### Printing darkroom under amber safelight

- **Audience identification:** low to medium. Fixed stations and irreversible handover map to commissioning, but the darkroom is not a known SHK ritual.
- **Product clarity:** medium. Sequential work can explain the service, but amber-on-black makes an ordinary local service feel like a niche instrument app.
- **Verdict:** loses to Inbetriebnahme-Pass on both axes.

### Multiplane cel-animation dawn

- **Audience identification:** low. A layered house/landscape can suggest comfort, but painted forests do not prove technical competence.
- **Product clarity:** medium. Strong phone composition and visual depth; weak service specificity without overlaid explanation.
- **Verdict:** loses on identification and truth, despite a useful mobile composition lesson.

### Art Deco cinema lobby

- **Audience identification:** very low. Craft level is high, but premiere ritual and brass glamour are culturally unrelated to most SHK operators.
- **Product clarity:** low. Decorative framing competes with service, region and phone.
- **Verdict:** fuses poorly because it turns a practical home-service decision into event theatre.

## Standing canon

A familiar high-end German SHK website: white/navy shell, real technician hero, services visible, phone action, three service sections and regional contact. This remains the user's permanent safe exit, not the recommendation.
