2. **Regionale Gewerbezeitung** — local advertising pages and editorial comparison make offers scannable but can feel promotional before premium.
3. **Materialmuster-Wand** — authored surfaces demonstrate craft and variety but do not explain the conversion mechanism as directly.
4. **Schaufenster-Leitsystem** — cut-vinyl glass lettering, fascia signs and a lit interior make the website itself the local business's visible storefront. **Assigned by roll.**
5. **Innungszeichen-System** — plaques, marks and certification grammar communicate trust but risk implying credentials BELIVIN cannot claim.
6. **Städtisches Gewerbe-Leitsystem** — address plaques and directional signs clarify route and action but identify place more than website quality.
7. **Regionaler Handwerksatlas** — plotted routes and indexed trades express local reach but weaken the immediate Website-Check action.
